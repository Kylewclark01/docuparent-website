Explore PPDS

Each section has a clear purpose, so parents can quickly understand where information belongs without navigating a complicated system.

New Entry

Document an event, observation, concern, or important parenting moment while the details are still fresh.

Communications Hub

Keep texts, emails, phone calls, screenshots, and related context together instead of scattered across different apps and devices.

Evidence Vault

Store photos, records, court documents, school information, medical files, and other supporting evidence in one organized location.

Reminders

Keep track of exchanges, appointments, follow-ups, deadlines, and documentation that still needs your attention.

Parenting Plan & Orders

Keep parenting plans and court orders available for quick reference alongside the records and events they relate to.

Clarity Assistant

Keep your original wording while creating an additional version that is clearer, more neutral, and easier for professionals to review.

Import Center

Bring in existing files in batches, review them, and organize them into PPDS—especially useful when documentation already spans months or years.

Case Builder

Turn your organized records into a useful final product. Case Builder groups similar incidents together, shows when each occurred, and connects the supporting evidence that helps document the pattern or issue.

The goal is a clear, organized file for review—not another place to re-enter information.
Clarity Assistant

When emotions are high, your documentation can still be clear.

Clarity Assistant helps you review wording for neutrality and readability while preserving your original entry.

Original entry
“She was late again and clearly doesn’t care about being on time for exchanges.”
✦
Clarity Assistant version
“The scheduled exchange was at 4:00 PM. The other parent arrived at 4:27 PM.”
Your original wording stays preserved. Clarity Assistant gives you an additional neutral version to use when helpful.

Planned Feature

Professional Access

Future versions of PPDS are planned to allow a parent to grant an authorized professional access to selected case information when deeper review is needed.

Parent-controlled accessThe parent chooses whether access is granted and what information is shared.
Evidence available when neededA professional could move from the quick-reference summary to selected supporting files and complete entries.
Less back-and-forthOrganized access can reduce repeated requests for screenshots, documents, and explanations that are already stored in PPDS.
Professional Access is a planned capability and is not currently represented as a live feature. The parent remains the owner of the case information and controls authorization.
