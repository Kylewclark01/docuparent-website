The Problem

Important information gets scattered everywhere.

Texts. Emails. School updates. Medical records. Photos. Parenting plans. Notes about exchanges and events. When information lives across phones, inboxes, folders, and memory, creating a clear record becomes exhausting.

PPDS is designed to give parents one structured place to document, preserve, organize, and prepare that information.

“I didn't realize how difficult parenting documentation could become until I had to manage it myself. PPDS grew from the need for a simpler, more organized way to preserve important information.”

— Founder story

How It Works

Document less chaotically. Prepare more clearly.

1. CaptureRecord an event, communication, or supporting item.
2. OrganizeKeep information connected by date, category, and supporting evidence.
3. ClarifyUse Clarity Assistant when you want a more neutral, fact-focused version.
4. PrepareSelect the relevant records and build an organized case summary.
For Professionals

Help clients arrive better organized.

PPDS is being designed to help parents prepare relevant information before it reaches attorneys, therapists, medical providers, and other professionals — reducing the time spent reconstructing scattered records.
